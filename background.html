Background

Over 15 years of engineering across wind energy, healthcare, agriculture, autonomous driving and the public sector, the last decade focused on AI and machine learning. Most recently AI Engineer and Advisor at the KI-Servicezentrum Berlin-Brandenburg of the Hasso Plattner Institute: requirements analysis, prototyping, workshops and pilot projects with Generative AI, LLMs, RAG and Computer Vision, including locally run LLM tools for public administration. Before that, automated ICD coding of clinical documents with NLP at MIA Medical Information Analytics, and seven years developing pitch systems for wind turbines at Suzlon Energy, from electronics design to SCADA data analysis. Independent since 2018, taking machine learning solutions from first prototype to deployed service. Degree in Electronics Engineering (Universidad Nacional de Rosario), DAAD scholarship at TU Braunschweig. Working languages: English, German and Spanish.
